What a point means

The invariants refer to the full twisted period lattice Γ. A character of order d gives d poles in an ordinary period cell. The atlas includes ordinary waves and characters of orders two through six.

Off the cusp the lattice is elliptic. On the cusp it degenerates; its origin is the rational limit. The figures count meromorphic waves with real lattice invariants, modulo translation and phase. A real lattice does not alone guarantee a smooth, bounded real-valued physical profile.

Each panel declares a particular coefficient slice. In the two highest quadratic cases and the twisted cubic sixth-order case, several branches at the same slider value generally specify different remaining coefficients. Their displayed count is a count of compatible equations. The conservative p = 2 equations have a free energy and carry infinitely many waves.

This atlas treats pure-power nonlinearities. Equations with nonlinear derivative terms form a separate class.
