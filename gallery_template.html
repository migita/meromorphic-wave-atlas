A little geometry goes a long way

How to read the atlas.

A curve is a family

Select anywhere on a curve to choose its parameter and branch. The wave, equation and readouts follow together. Colors run from the sweep’s beginning to its end.

The cusp is a limit

On Δ = g₂³ − 27g₃² = 0, an elliptic lattice degenerates. A real pole-free slice can approach a solitary pulse or a constant. Real invariants alone do not guarantee a smooth real wave.

Know what varies

KdV’s energy and C4’s free wave coordinates can vary within one equation. Kawahara’s dispersion and KS’s integration constant change the equation. Higher-order compatibility branches can represent different equations.
